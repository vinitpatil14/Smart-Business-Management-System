Abstract

In modern commercial environments, small and medium enterprises (SMEs) face operational friction caused by disconnected systems for inventory auditing, point-of-sale checkout, customer balance ledgering, overhead expense categorization, and employee payroll processing. Traditional enterprise software solutions remain expensive, desktop-tethered, and complex.

The Smart Business Management System (SBMS / NexPulse Business OS) addresses these challenges by delivering an omnichannel, responsive, progressive web application designed with high-velocity UI architecture. Powered by React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Node.js Express, Prisma ORM, and integrated with Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore, the system bridges local POS execution with real-time cloud data telemetry. The application incorporates a specialized Zero-Latency Client-Side Engine ensuring that Point of Sale checkout, printable invoice generation, stock deduction, and attendance marking operate seamlessly with 100% availability even under static hosting environments like Vercel.

Chapter 1: Introduction & Project Overview

1.1 Project Background

Commercial retail enterprises and wholesale merchants require instantaneous, dependable computing infrastructure to conduct counter-top sales, monitor inventory levels across warehouses, track customer credit balances (Khata), record overhead expenditures, and manage employee salaries. Disconnected point solutions create data discrepancies, leading to stockouts, revenue leakage, and uncollected debt.

1.2 Problem Statement

Most existing Point of Sale and ERP applications suffer from key architectural deficiencies:

  • Complex Desktop Installation: Dependence on local database servers requiring dedicated maintenance and lack of mobile access.
  • Poor Device Responsiveness: Interfaces break or overlap on tablet and mobile phone screens used by traveling managers and shop clerks.
  • Lack of Real-Time Analytics: Store owners cannot visualize 14-day sales trajectories, category profitability, or stockout timelines.
  • Manual Debt Collection: Merchants spend considerable manual effort drafting and sending credit balance reminders to customers.

1.3 Project Objectives

  • Develop a 100% responsive Single Page Application (SPA) accessible across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
  • Implement high-speed barcode-capable POS billing with multi-tier GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and immediate printable tax invoices.
  • Incorporate real-time stock deductions, automatic reorder threshold alerts, and inventory archiving.
  • Provide interactive staff attendance tracking and automated monthly payroll calculations.
  • Deliver an AI-powered Smart Engine computing demand burn-rate and automated WhatsApp reminder generation.

Chapter 2: System Architecture & Tech Stack

The system employs a modern decoupled client-server architecture designed for high availability, zero latency, and multi-cloud resilience.

Core Architectural Layers:
  • Frontend Presentation Layer: React 18, Vite 5, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Recharts SVG Engine.
  • Authentication & Identity Layer: Google Firebase Authentication (Google OAuth 2.0 & Email/Password Sign-In).
  • Cloud Data Layer: Google Cloud Firestore NoSQL Database for real-time document synchronization.
  • Relational Backend Layer: Node.js, Express.js REST API, Prisma ORM, SQLite / PostgreSQL.
  • Offline & Cloud Standalone Engine: Client-side in-memory and persistent LocalStorage store ensuring 0ms latency and 100% uptime on CDN deployments (Vercel, Netlify).

Chapter 3: Functional Modules & Specifications

3.1 Executive Telemetry Dashboard

The dashboard aggregates operational performance metrics in real time. It features:

  • Four Primary Telemetry Cards: Today's Sales Volume & Bill Count, Monthly Cumulative Revenue, Estimated Net Profit (After COGS and Overhead Deductions), and Total Customer Outstanding Dues.
  • Interactive 14-Day Sales Trend Line Chart: Recharts-powered visualization displaying daily invoice throughput.
  • Category Revenue Distribution Pie Chart: Proportional visual analysis across merchandise categories.
  • 6-Month Revenue vs. Operating Expenses Bar Chart: Comparative overview tracking cash generation against operational costs.
  • Watchlist & Alerts: Instant visual indicators for low-stock SKUs and overdue customer payments.

3.2 Inventory & Warehouse Control

The inventory engine manages stock quantities, cost bases, and reorder levels. Features include:

  • Comprehensive SKU tracking with multi-unit measurement (kg, pcs, tin, jar, box, pack).
  • Dual-table layout supporting active inventory and soft-deleted archived products.
  • Automated color-coded status badges: In Stock (Green), Low Stock (Amber), Out of Stock (Red).
  • Dynamic category categorization with product count rollups.

3.3 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Manages commercial buyer profiles, contact books, outstanding receivables balances, and loyalty points. Seamlessly interfaces with the POS system to record on-credit sales and update individual credit histories.

Chapter 4: Point of Sale (POS) & Invoicing Engine

The Point of Sale (POS) system represents the transactional heartbeat of NexPulse Business OS, engineered to process fast counter sales with zero friction.

4.1 POS Features & Workflow

  • Live Catalog Grid: Category-filtered, instant search by product name or SKU with visual stock counter badges.
  • Active Order Basket: Dynamic quantity adjustment, item removal, and auto-calculation of subtotal.
  • GST Tax Engine: Configurable standard GST tax rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) with automatic tax separation.
  • Discounts & Adjustments: Direct rupee discount entry with auto-deduction from the gross total.
  • Flexible Payment Modes: Support for Cash, UPI / QR, Credit/Debit Card, and Credit (Udhaar).

4.2 Tax Invoice Generation

Upon clicking "Complete Sale & Print Invoice", the transaction is finalized, inventory stock is decremented in real time, and a compliant Retail Tax Invoice is displayed in a dedicated preview modal.

Chapter 5: Human Resources & Payroll System

To alleviate the burden of managing staff rosters and monthly salary calculations, NexPulse includes an integrated Human Resources and Payroll subsystem.

5.1 Employee Directory Management

Enables the administrative creation, editing, and deletion of staff profiles. Tracked attributes include Full Name, Official Email, Phone Number, Designation (e.g., Operations Manager, Lead Cashier, Inventory Officer), Department, Base Monthly Salary, and Active/Inactive Status.

5.2 Daily Interactive Attendance System

Shop owners and managers can record employee attendance daily via an interactive matrix:

  • Date Selection: Calendar picker to select any target work day.
  • One-Click Status Buttons:
    • Present (Green): Credited as a full working day (1.0).
    • Half Day (Amber): Credited as half working day (0.5).
    • Leave (Blue): Authorized absence with tracking.
    • Absent (Red): Unauthorized absence (0.0).
  • Real-time State Persistence: Attendance marks are stored per date key and persist across sessions.

5.3 Automated Payroll Calculation

The payroll engine computes gross compensation based on actual days worked:

Payroll Formula:
Payable Days = Present Days + (Half Days × 0.5)
Net Payable Salary = (Base Salary / 26 Working Days) × Payable Days

Chapter 6: Smart AI Business Intelligence

NexPulse Business OS integrates algorithmic business intelligence to transform historical transaction logs into actionable operational insights.

6.1 Demand Burn-Rate Stockout Predictor

The system analyzes moving 14-day sales velocity to compute the daily consumption rate (units/day) for every catalog SKU:

Daily Burn Rate = Total Units Sold (Past 14 Days) / 14
Days Remaining = Current Stock Quantity / Daily Burn Rate

Based on the computed days remaining, SKUs are classified into risk categories: Critical (<3 days), High Risk (3–7 days), Moderate Risk (7–14 days), and Safe (15+ days), providing automated recommended reorder quantities.

6.2 Product Velocity & Dead Stock Detection

  • Best Performers: Top products by sales volume and total revenue generation.
  • High-Margin Contributors: Items delivering maximum margin percentage per unit sold.
  • Dead Stock Watchlist: Identifies inventory units with zero sales over 30 days, calculating tied-up capital and suggesting clearance actions.

6.3 Automated WhatsApp Payment Reminders

For customers with outstanding credit balances, the engine generates personalized, polite collection messages ready to dispatch via WhatsApp or SMS with one click.

Chapter 8: Security & Role-Based Access Control

8.1 Multi-Tier Authentication Architecture

The platform enforces secure authentication leveraging Google Firebase Authentication SDK:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 Sign-In: Verified identity tokens issued directly by Google accounts with popup authentication.
  • Email & Password Authentication: Password encryption and credentials managed by Firebase Auth backend.
  • Authorized Domain Whitelisting: Strict origin validation preventing cross-site token interception.

8.2 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Three standardized privilege tiers govern access to application functionality:

Role Access Permissions & Privileges
ADMIN Full platform control: View all financial metrics, edit inventory pricing, manage staff accounts, delete records, configure settings.
MANAGER Operational control: Inventory updates, purchase order creation, expense logging, attendance marking, report viewing.
STAFF Frontline operations: Sales POS billing, invoice generation, customer creation, stock lookup.

8.3 Data Segregation: Real Users vs. Demo Showroom

A critical architectural achievement is the complete segregation between pre-configured One-Click Demo accounts and newly registered Real Users:

  • Demo Showroom: Provides a fully populated store environment (products, charts, sales history) for evaluation without manual data entry.
  • Real User Workspace: Real users receive an isolated, zero-state workspace (sbms_user_<uid>_*). Their commercial transactions remain private and are never contaminated by demo records.
